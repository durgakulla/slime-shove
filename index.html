How to play

  • Drag to slide the slime along the line, then let go to shove it up the board.
  • Two slimes of the same size merge into the next size up.
  • Each extra merge from a single shove pays more: 2nd ×2, 3rd ×3. Keep every shove merging to build a streak bonus.
  • It ends when slimes settle past the overflow line.

Powers about 1 shove in 30

Modes

Classic is a long board; Rush is shorter and faster. Each keeps its own best score.
